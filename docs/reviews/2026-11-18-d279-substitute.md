# Review: the substituted waypoint and the uncapped ground re-plan - built, green alone, not enough for the slide

*Change (branch `d279-substitute`): `Route::waited` counts consecutive **ground refusals on the current waypoint** (hashed);
past `SUBSTITUTE_STALLS` the waypoint is **dropped** rather than waited on, and `MAX_GROUND_REPLANS` is gone from the ground
branch - the original counts nothing per route and substitutes instead. The terrain slide is deleted on this branch too, as
the slice it was meant to unblock. **Not for `main`**: with the slide gone the suite is red, and the numbers below say the
substitution is not what the slide needs.*

## Performance

Measured on this branch, `cargo test -p ruage-sim --no-fail-fast`:

- **substitution + cap removal, slide still in**: animals **11/11**, `repath_budget` **4/4**, `repath_reasons` **6/6**, `stall`,
  `stuck`, `movement`, `collision`, `gather_motion` all green - **0 failures in 8 binaries**, animals in **5.66 s**.
- **and then the slide deleted**: animals **8/3** (the same three sheep fixtures) and `gather` **20/2**
  (`a_depleted_node_stops_yielding`, `a_partial_load_comes_home_when_the_node_runs_out`), the two binaries taking **17.02 s** and
  **28.02 s** against 5.66 s and ~6 s - the runs spend their budget in refusals, which is the signature D135 taught to read.

## Stability

**What was attacked.** The counter is hashed (`Route::waited`), reset by any step that happens and by any waypoint that is spent,
and the **goal is never dropped** (`points.len() > 1`), so an unreachable goal still ends standing - `a_walled_in_goal` stays
green.

**What the two runs prove.** The substitution is **real and landable on its own** - it changes nothing that was green - and it is
**not sufficient for the slide**: dropping the first waypoint only aims the man at the *second* corner of the same unwalkable
route. That is D259's finding again from the other side: the route is not the problem, the leg from where the body stands is.

**Found and reported:** this slice was written **before its fixture**, which breaks the project's first rule. No fixture was
watched failing for the substitution itself; the evidence is that the existing suites stayed green, which proves the change is
inert where they look, not that it does what the game does. The fixture it owes - a waypoint that a re-plan keeps returning and
that the mover therefore never reaches - is not written.

Known and accepted: parked, not merged. `main` is green.
