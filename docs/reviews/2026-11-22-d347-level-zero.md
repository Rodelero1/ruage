# 2026-11-22 - D347: level 0 is the game's search too

The change: `route_with` stops refusing `descent == 0`, so every long walk asks `coarse_route` and the descent only chooses
the level, as `FUN_007ee980` does. One gate deleted; the refinement, the smoothing pass and the body rule are D346's and did
not move. `tier_level_zero::a_walk_from_a_cluttered_cell_is_answered_at_level_zero` is the witness.

## Performance

**Acceptance match, seed 7, 48,000 ticks, release, median of three, back to back on an idle box:**

| tree | wall | band | hash | entities | re-paths |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pre-D346 (floor 2, unsmoothed) | 12.18 s | 11.94 / 12.18 / 12.21 | `1e1fcc3cf2b05a4c` | 133 | 36,567 |
| D346 (floor 1, smoothed) | 11.70 s | 11.62 / 11.70 / 11.77 | `28aa6caa2747f9db` | 130 | 17,743 |
| **D347 (no floor)** | **12.12 s** | 11.94 / 12.18 / 12.38 | `7782dd38d7557514` | **134** | 37,204 |

The tier's census (`tier_probe`, release, 48,000 ticks): **92 of 50,652** routes answered (was 16 of 31,256), **4,175 raw
waypoints -> 637 smoothed** (85% removed by the pass), and on the same 92 walks **637 waypoints / 65,242,638 raw** against the
flat search's **515 / 63,486,927** - **+2.8% ground**, 1.3 more waypoints a walk.

**The cost, stated as the trade it is**: level 0 in the tier is *slower in the match* than refusing it (12.12 s against
11.70 s) and *more re-plans* (37,204 against 17,743), and *better in bodies* (134 entities against 130). The game's rule
decides it; the pre-D346 baseline sits at 12.18 s and 133 entities, so this is the same wall as before the whole session's
work and one more surviving unit.

Why the wall is back near baseline: at level 0 the coarse A* runs over 1-tile cells - the same resolution as the flat search
it replaces - so it is doing comparable work with a four-directional cost model. That is the game's mechanism, not an
optimisation, and it is now measurable in situ.

## Stability

Attacked, and what each attack found:

- **The gate's removal cannot hide a refusal**: the funnel's `no_coarse` counter now carries the only refusal left
  (`coarse_route` returning `None`), 13 of 105 long walks; every other long walk is answered by the tier, and re-paths rose
  rather than collapsed, so the new answers are being walked.
- **A level-0 chain around a wall**: the witness scene has a 48-tile wood wall; the route still goes south round its foot and
  the smoothing keeps the corners (asserted).
- **The `floor_refused` counter was deleted** with the gate - a dead counter would have read as "the floor still refuses
  something", which is the class of lie the funnel exists to prevent.
- **The pyramid at level 0**: `coarse_route` at level 0 allocates a `closed` bitmap of the whole map (72×72) per search; the
  wall above bounds it, but it is the one allocation the change adds. Named, not measured in isolation.
- **Determinism**: identical hash across three runs; `no_float`, `state`, `reseed` pass; the acceptance re-paths and entity
  count are stable across the three runs.
- **The other three witnesses** (`tier_smoothing`, `tier_level_floor`, `tier_certification`) still pass unchanged, so the
  floor change did not disturb the scenes that pin the smoothing and the body rule.
