# No pushing at a resource, and what it does to the match

**Branch `d204-no-pushing`, one commit.** D204 is built: a man who cannot get his square at a node takes the next tile
of the same resource, and no body that is gathering is moved by another body. The change is `crates/sim/tests/no_pushing.rs`
(two behaviour tests, watched red first) plus the gather arm's refusal count, `square_refused`, and two gates in
`push_the_standing`.

## What changed, and what was measured before it

| | `main` (`7d00be8`) | this branch |
|---|---|---|
| acceptance summary | `entities=143 players=2 hash=2d93236ebb16961b repaths=373 made_room=140 repaths_body=77 repaths_ground=296` | `entities=160 players=2 hash=c3b10f4b09e17a43 repaths=324 made_room=147 repaths_body=83 repaths_ground=241` |
| food at 400..2400 s | `4 / 14 / 484 / 594 / 1226 / 1396` | `45 / 49 / 268 / 120 / 660 / 450` |
| wood at 400..2400 s | `40 / 75 / 300 / 415 / 805 / 1215` | `80 / 96 / 135 / 75 / 395 / 185` |
| units at 400..2400 s | `20 / 29 / 33 / 37 / 37 / 37` | `20 / 31 / 35 / 44 / 46 / 48` |
| `give-away` counters | `made_room=140` | `made_room=147` |

Same command, same seed (`--seed 7 --ticks 48000 --report 400`), both binaries built `--release` from their own trees and
measured back to back.

**The reading, stated plainly.** The branch **ends with eleven more units** (48 against 37) and **less in the bank**
(food 450 against 1,396, wood 185 against 1,215): it converts the wood into villagers and buildings rather than leaving
it lying about. `buildings` is 30 against 23 and `gold` reaches 300 at 2,000 s against 10. At 400 s the branch banks
**45 food where `main` banks 4** - the early game that D200's crowd policy was costing - and the guard
(`crates/sim/tests/acceptance_economy.rs`) is green in both of its tests.

**What the nudge was actually doing, measured on `main`** before any code moved, by printing the task of every body it
displaced over the acceptance run: **310 displacements - 232 builders, 76 loaded carriers walking home, 2 idle bodies,
and 0 gatherers.** So the owner's "villagers pushing villagers" in a real match was builders and carriers, not men at a
node: the acceptance map never had a gather body pushed. What it *did* have is the other half of D204 - **45,359
refusals of a square**, over **18 distinct villagers**, one of them refused on **1,782 consecutive ticks** while the
same resource column had nodes with room. That is the state this change removes, and it is why the fixture is a crowd
round one tree rather than a staged shove.

## Performance

- **Acceptance wall time, 40 minutes of game time: `main` 61.3 s -> this branch 30.3 s** (two runs each: 61.3/61.3 and
  30.3/30.6). The two runs are *not the same match* (the hash differs), so this is an end-to-end number and part of it
  is the different path, not a cheaper tick. Said here so nobody quotes it as a tick cost.
- **The like-for-like tick cost**, `perf::a_ten_minute_match_in_release` (same map, same 128 entities, 12,000 ticks,
  three runs each): `main` **7.4 / 7.4 / 8.4 us/tick**, this branch **6.5 / 6.7 / 6.8 us/tick**. The mechanism is the
  state above: a refused man re-runs `standoff_point` (the whole `touching_places` geometry plus a body scan per
  candidate) on every tick he stands there, and on this branch he stops standing there.
- **The cost the change adds** is one `nearest_like_free` search per man per forty refused ticks. It is bounded by the
  same constant the displacement rule used (`STALLED_TICKS`), only fires for a man who already has no square, and in the
  acceptance run `repaths` *fell* (373 -> 324) rather than rose.

## Stability - what was attacked

- **The D200 nudge still works where D204 keeps it.** `gather_motion::a_gatherer_walks_round_a_friend_standing_in_his_path`
  (a gatherer who must get past an idle body) is green: the rule is scoped to *the body being moved* (never a body with a
  gather order) and *the shover being a man refused a square at a node*, not to "a gatherer may never nudge".
- **The whole workspace: 185 test binaries, 843 passed, 0 failed, 21 ignored** (`cargo test --offline --workspace`,
  log `/opt/data/cache/scratch/d204/tests-after.log`). `main` had 184 / 841 / 0 / 21; the two new tests and the one new
  test file account for the difference. `fmt --check` clean, `clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` clean.
- **The failure no test covered, hunted**: a man re-targeted onto a *further* node whose ring is also full would ping-pong.
  `nearest_like_free` skips nodes whose places are taken (`claimed_by_other`) and skips the node he is leaving, and the
  count is reset to zero when the search finds nothing, so the next look is another `STALLED_TICKS` away rather than
  every tick. A man who is *seated* clears his count (the `else` branch), so a man who walks to his place and works is
  never charged for the wait at the node he left.
- **Determinism**: `square_refused` is a `BTreeMap` keyed by entity, read only against the man's current task node, and
  like `stalled`, `respread_at` and `gather_place` it is bookkeeping rather than hashed state - two runs of the seed gave
  the same hash (`c3b10f4b09e17a43`) twice. `no_float` is green: the count is `u32` and the fixture's arithmetic is in the
  test, not the engine.

## Withdrawn, and what killed it

**A gate on the shover's task** - "a man with a gather order never nudges anybody" - was written first, because it is the
simplest reading of *"I don't like villagers pushing other villagers"*. It was killed by the suite:
`gather_motion::a_gatherer_walks_round_a_friend_standing_in_his_path` went red with

```
MEASURED the gatherer never reached the bush in 1,500 ticks; closest 1.84 tiles at (8.16,6.71), last step Some(WaitBlocked)
```

- that test is a gatherer reaching his node past an idle body, and the nudge is the only thing that gets him there
  (D200's own note: the repath is refused while the body is in the route's avoid set). D204 keeps the nudge for a body
  with **no work at all**, which is exactly the body in that test, so the gate was wrong and was replaced by the two
  narrower ones: the shover must be a man *refused a square at a node* (`square_refused`), and the body moved must not
  have a gather order.
