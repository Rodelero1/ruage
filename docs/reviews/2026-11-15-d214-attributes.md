# Review: attribute 1 is sight and 12 is range - a live wrong number in a shipped upgrade

*Change: the importer's `ATTRIBUTE_NAMES` said attribute **1** was `range` and had no entry for **12** at all; the dat says 1
is line of sight (Town Watch/Town Patrol, +4 for classes 3 and 52) and 12 is attack range (Fletching's line, Greek Fire).
Because the pack's names are the engine's interface - `modifiers_for(player, unit, "range")` matches on the string - the
importer's mistake was a simulation bug: Town Watch moved a watch tower's **attack range** 8 -> 12 and left its sight at 10,
and Greek Fire's +1 range matched nothing. Fixed in the importer (T1a, two dict entries) and in the engine (T1b: the
classifier, one `effective_los` consumer at the three raw `los` reads, and a panel that had been showing base numbers under
a doc comment saying "Effective, not base"). D214.*

## Performance

**A measured regression - and a measurement error of my own - in that order.** The first A/B ran at 48,000 ticks, interleaved:
`8735cde` **42,151 / 42,033 ms** against the change **56,817 / 56,135 ms**, **+35%**, every run. That figure was wrong: I was
writing these notes and running `plan_check.py` and `decisions.py` (220 decisions parsed, a 354 KB page written) while the two
tree runs were in flight, and the reference runs fell in the quiet halves. A quiet re-measurement - three interleaved pairs at
24,000 ticks, nothing else on the box - gave `8735cde` **20,641 / 20,367 / 20,596 ms** (mean **20,535**) against the change
**22,097 / 21,766 / 22,181 ms** (mean **22,015**): **+7.2%**, with every pair in the same direction, which is a real cost.

**Where it was, measured rather than assumed.** `sight_of` runs for every body that moved, every tick, and `effective_los`
asked `modifiers_for`, which walks every researched tech and does a table lookup into the effect list for each one. Reverting
`sight_of` to the raw `los` alone restored the old figure while the panel and the re-stamp stayed in place, so the scan was
the whole of it. The fix is a per-player list of line-of-sight modifiers, rebuilt in `finish_research` - the one place a
bought tech and a granted tech both land - and when the civ bonuses are set, the only two things that can add one.
`effective_los` now returns `base.raw()` on one branch when the list is empty, which is every player with no sight tech.

**After the fix**, the same quiet protocol, three pairs at 24,000 ticks: `8735cde` **20,426 / 20,349 / 20,548 ms** (mean
**20,441**) against **20,699 / 20,573 / 20,445 ms** (mean **20,572**) - **+0.6%**, inside the 1.0% spread of the reference
side and unresolved by this measurement.

**What this change cannot move.** The fog the renderer reads is `visible_mask` - one pass per frame over the sight discs -
which gained one cached lookup per entity and no per-tile work. The acceptance match is **byte-identical**
(`hash=42212c8e852b62be`, `entities=144`, `repaths=380` at 48,000 ticks; `435c46a1dc82f34d` at 24,000), so what moved is what
a test can see, not what this script does.

## Stability

What this change could break, and what pins each part:

- **A rename is a behaviour change.** The words in the pack are matched by string, so `range` -> `line_of_sight` had to
  arrive with a consumer or it would have made sight upgrades inert in a *different* way. Two importer tests were written
  first and watched failing: `test_attribute_one_is_line_of_sight_and_twelve_is_attack_range` (attribute 1 and 12 through
  `effect_record`) and `test_no_two_attribute_ids_share_a_name` (a duplicate name merges two attributes into one consumer,
  silently). Measured red: `['range', 'range', 'other:12']`.
- **The pack really changed.** Regenerated against the shipped `empires2_x2_p1.dat`: 40 modifiers moved from `range` to
  `line_of_sight`, 26 from `other:12` to `range`, `other:12` left at zero. `civs.toml`, `techs.toml` and `units.toml` differ
  only in the `# generated:` timestamp - the on-disk pack was otherwise already current with the importer, which is why this
  one rename is the whole data delta.
- **Every sight path, not one.** `los` was read raw at four sites; a single consumer would have left three of them base. The
  new test (`modifiers::town_watch_widens_a_building_s_sight_and_leaves_its_range_alone`) asserts the panel's numbers
  (`los` 1000 -> 1400, range 800 unchanged) **and** the fog (`visible_to` for an enemy twelve tiles out), which is the
  behaviour rather than the number.
- **The defect the fix uncovered.** The fog assertion failed *after* the sight arithmetic was right: exploration is stamped
  per **move** and a building is stamped once, at placement, so a sight upgrade marked no new ground and `vision` answered
  *unseen* for a tile the tower could now watch. `restamp_player` re-stamps a player's entities when a tech lands - once per
  research, idempotent when the tech carries no sight. This is the third time in this run of work that a number was right
  and the mechanism behind it was not.
- **Zero stays zero.** `effective_los` returns 0 for a unit whose `los` does not resolve or is not positive, before the
  modifiers run: a "+4 sight" on a blind unit must not become a four-tile radius.

Known and accepted:

- `seed.rs`'s per-unit sight table (livestock flee radii) still resolves `los` raw. It is keyed by unit id rather than by
  player, and it is the *animal's* sight, not the owner's; a player's sight tech must not widen it. Recorded so the next
  reader does not "fix" it.
- `ATTRIBUTE_NAMES` still leaves 13 of the dat's attributes unnamed (`other:N`). They are inert by construction and this
  change does not widen the named set beyond 1 and 12.
