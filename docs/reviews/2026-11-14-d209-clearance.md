# D209 - the smaller pencil: routes bend round a body inside a tile

**Built, working in isolation, and parked with the triage below.** The mechanism the last three slices were missing; the
remaining failures are three obsolete tests, three gather regressions I have not explained, and the rest of the same class.

## What it is

`short_range_clear(walk, scale, from, to, radius, avoid_tiles, bodies)` - the same search as `short_range`, with two
changes:

- **bodies are discs, not tiles**: `bodies` is `(centre, clearance)` and the route keeps `clearance` clear of each centre,
  tested exactly in integers (`cross^2 >= clearance^2 * length^2` - no division, no sqrt, i128);
- **eight shoulder points** per body at the clearance, so a route can bend to the left or right of a man *inside* one tile,
  which a tile-corner graph cannot express.

The clearance is `my radius + his` - the distance the original grows its obstacle rectangles by - and **exactly that, with no
margin** (see the error below). A route may always *leave* a disc it already stands inside, or a man who has just met
somebody would have no legal first step.

`stander_bodies` feeds it: every unit that did **not** move on the last tick, excluding the start and goal tiles. That is the
original's rule for its per-search rectangles (`FUN_007f9d80`: insert iff not ignored and velocity is zero), and the tile set
is kept alongside it for the fallback search that can only think in tiles.

## The whole gate, and an honest triage

`cargo test --workspace --no-fail-fast`: **190 binaries, 835 passed, 18 failed, 22 ignored.** Of those 18:

- **3 are tests that pin the rule D208 replaced** - `footprint::a_head_on_pair_in_a_one_tile_corridor_never_overlaps`,
  `group_overlap::two_units_ordered_apart_stay_solid`, `group_overlap::a_mate_who_is_stopped_becomes_solid`. Under D208 two
  men who meet **do** pass each other (measured closest 151 raw, and 4207 raw after a stop). They must be rewritten to
  D208's outcome - both arrive, the loser pauses 0.25-1.50 s - not "never overlap";
- **3 are a real regression I have not explained**: `gather::a_depleted_node_stops_yielding` reports the node **untouched**
  (`left: Fixed(100000)`, i.e. full), and `a_partial_load_comes_home_when_the_node_runs_out` and
  `two_villagers_on_one_node_cannot_take_more_than_it_holds` fail with it. A worker is not harvesting at all - most likely a
  plan that now avoids a standing neighbour and never reaches the square, but **not established**;
- **12 more** in the same families (routing, rally, a replan-route pull test that now sees non-tile waypoints). Each needs
  the same treatment: establish whether the expectation is pre-D209 or the change is wrong.

## Performance

Not measured this round, and that is a gap: the D208 half measured 2.3x slower and D209 changes the search again, so the
number has to be taken before this lands. The cost this change adds per plan is eight nodes per standing body and a disc
test per edge; the reason it must still be measured is that D208's 2.3x came from *failed* searches, and a search that
succeeds more often may pay for itself.

## Stability

- **Exact integers only**, no float and no sqrt; `clears_disc` is a pure predicate and `contains_disc` is the only licence to
  move *out* of a disc.
- **Deterministic:** bodies come from state (positions and `last_moved`), the disc and node order is fixed by iteration order,
  and the shoulder points are computed, never drawn.
- **Two of my own errors, both found by measurement:** the first `stander_bodies` filter skipped **every body in a fresh
  world** (`last_moved + 1 >= tick` is true at tick 1 when the sentinel `0` means "never moved"), so plans kept answering
  with a straight line through a man standing there since the seed; and a one-eighth-tile clearance margin, added to stop the
  mover cutting the circle, put every neighbour's place inside a standing worker's disc and broke the gather ring (18 tests).
  Both are fixed - the margin is gone, "never moved" is standing.
