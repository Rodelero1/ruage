# Review: a farm's replacement is queued or automatic, and the setting is the player's

*Change: `Command::Reseed` and `Command::AutoReseed`, both pressed at a mill of the player's, writing `reseed: BTreeMap<player, u32>`
and `auto_reseed: BTreeSet<player>`; a paid-site path; and a husk that releases its ground. D226.*

## Performance

**Off the per-tick path entirely**: the cost is one `BTreeMap`/`BTreeSet` lookup per plot that *husks* (a farm lasts minutes), plus a
short walk of the farm's cost list, and the commands' price check. Nothing new runs per tick - `step_husks` already visits the tick's
new husks every fourth tick. Measured: the reseed suite's four fixtures run in **1.9 s**, and the acceptance match's entity count and
hash are unchanged (`beb6c66f58e4045c`, 154 entities at 24,000 ticks) because the acceptance scenario reseeds nothing, so the two new
maps are empty and hash nothing.

## Stability

- **The setting is keyed by player, never by building** - the owner's correction, and the thing a per-mill implementation would have
  got wrong quietly. The fixture presses the toggle at a mill in the far corner and reseeds a plot beside the town centre, so
  "shared across every mill" is asserted rather than described.
- **Both pieces are in the state hash.** A run that lost the queue would leave plots to go to seed while the other replaced them: a
  divergence with no other symptom. The map is hashed sorted by player and only when non-empty, so the acceptance hash of a match that
  never reseeds does not move.
- **The price is charged only after the site is raised.** A paid site whose ground is refused - which is exactly what happened first,
  `SiteBlocked` on the husk - would otherwise take 60 wood for nothing. The queue entry survives a refusal and is spent on the next
  plot.
- **`start_paid_site` relaxes the price and nothing else.** Availability (mill built, civ allowed), the site's ground and the walk are
  still checked, so the cheapest possible bug - "paid, therefore unchecked" - cannot exist.
- **Two mutations, both bite.** Removing the `reseed_spent` hook fails both reseed fixtures; putting the husk's walk-block back fails
  both with `SiteBlocked`. The second is a genuine defect found by this slice: the engine's own doc said a husk is walkable and the walk
  layer disagreed.

Known and accepted:

- **No panel, no button**: there is no renderer, so the commands are the interface and the mill's role is the question a panel would
  ask. A script drives both today - that is how the fixtures prove them.
- **The replacement is handed to the nearest villager free to build**; the original may prefer the man who was on the plot, which in
  practice is the same man because he is standing on it. Not measured against a crowd, and the note says so.
- **Automatic reseeding is Definitive Edition behaviour**, built here on instruction; the queue is HD/AoC. Both are recorded as such so
  a later session does not "correct" one into the other.
